## Shipping Policy
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days.
Express delivery usually takes 1 to 3 business days.
Customers receive tracking information after the order is shipped.
Delivery times may vary during holidays or unexpected delays.

## Return Policy
Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original condition.
Certain products may require an inspection before a return is approved.
Damaged or incorrect items should be reported to customer support as soon as possible.

## Refund Policy
Approved refunds are processed after the returned product passes inspection.
Refund processing time depends on the original payment method.
Shipping charges may not be refundable in some cases.

## Payment Policy
We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards, and net banking.
If a payment is deducted but the order is not created, customers should contact support with the transaction details.

## Warranty Policy
Warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal usage.
Accidental damage, liquid damage, and unauthorized repairs are not covered.
Warranty claims may require proof of purchase and product inspection.

## Account Support
Customers can request help with login issues, account information, and password-related problems.
Sensitive account or payment information should not be shared in chat.

## Escalation Policy
A support case should be escalated to a human agent when:
1. The AI does not have enough information to answer.
2. The customer's request involves sensitive account or payment information.
3. The customer disputes a transaction or refund decision.
4. The customer reports a serious unresolved product issue.
5. The AI confidence is below the required threshold.

## Support Guidelines
- Give answers based on the available knowledge base.
- Do not invent policies, prices, order information, or guarantees.
- Ask for clarification when the customer's question is unclear.
- Escalate cases that cannot be safely resolved.
- Never request passwords, OTPs, or complete payment card details.
